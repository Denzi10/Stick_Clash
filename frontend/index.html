CONTROLS & HOW TO PLAY

Closing in 5s...
🏃 MOVEMENT & HIGH JUMPS
A D Move Left / Right
W or SPACE Super High Jump (Triple Jump!) Tap in air to reach high tiers
S Crouch / Drop Through Ledge
U or SHIFT Dash Dodge (Invincible frames)
⚔️ COMBAT & COMBOS
J Light Attack (Chain 3-hit combo)
K Heavy Attack (Hold to charge +50% DMG)
I Block & Parry (Time with enemy strike to STUN!)
O Pick Up Crate / Throw Weapon
⚡ POWER, SPECIALS & RAGE MODE
L Class Special (40 Power): Quake Slam / Shadow Dash / Blast / Tackle
R Rage Mode (100 Power): +30% Damage, +15% Speed, & HP Regen!

✨ Power charges automatically over time and with every punch landed!

📦 Smash crates to grab Swords, Staffs, Blasters & Shurikens.

TRAINING DOJO CONTROLS

Move / Crouch: A / D / S (Stick / Arrows)

Jump / Double Jump: W or Space (A on Gamepad)

Light Attack: J (Starts combo, chains up to 3 hits)

Heavy Attack: K (Hold to charge +50% damage)

Dash: U or Shift (Invulnerable during first frames)

Block & Parry: I (Within 8 frames to Parry & stun foe!)

Grab / Throw / Pickup: O

Special Move: L (Costs 50 power)

Rage Mode: R (Costs 100 power, +30% dmg, +15% spd, HP regen)
